# Unit 5 Finding: Two groups of matches, two different problems

## Matches that fail often: 2 and 3

Match 2 (APAC) and match 3 (OCE) lose players to network timeouts three to four times more often than a normal busy match (18.8% and 15.4% against 4.8%). They are played, but players get disconnected. Both include the Survival mode, but matches 5 and 10 also use Survival and had no timeouts, so the mode is not the cause; both are large 16-player matches, but match 5 is also 16 players and had no timeouts, so size alone does not explain it either. Recommendation: ask the server team to check the network and server logs for these two matches, and keep watching their timeout rate against the 4.8% norm. Match 4 (EU) looks high only because one timeout in 6 participations is already 16.7%; it should be monitored, not investigated.

## Matches that never started: 14 and 15

Match 14 (NA) and match 15 (SA) are in the catalogue but have no recorded participation at all, and no game mode assigned. They did not appear in earlier reports because those reports only counted matches with activity. The data does not tell us why: they may have been cancelled, never fully set up, or their activity may not have been recorded. Recommendation: ask the operations team to confirm whether these matches were cancelled or misconfigured. If they are not needed, remove them from the catalogue; if players did play and nothing was recorded, fix the data collection.

## Why the responses differ

Matches 2 and 3 are a quality problem: the matches run, but the connection fails. Matches 14 and 15 are an availability or setup problem: nothing was recorded at all, so there is no failure rate to fix, only a missing match to explain.
